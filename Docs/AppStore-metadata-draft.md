- **Promotional text**: Why is your iPhone hot? Vitals keeps a heat timeline and, with one shared log, names the apps
  that were busy when it happened. Everything stays on your phone.
- **Description**:
  A check-up for the iPhone in your hand. One tap reads battery, heat, storage, memory, network and software, and
  tells you in one sentence what is fine and what needs a look.

  Heat: iOS does not give apps a temperature, but it does say when the phone is warm, hot or very hot. Vitals records
  that every chance it gets, finds hot spells, and when you share that day's system reports it ranks the processes
  that were busiest at the time.

  Battery wear, crashes and memory pressure: share the analytics file iOS already keeps (Settings > Privacy &
  Security > Analytics & Improvements) and Vitals reads it on the phone.

  What to do: every finding comes with clear steps, the Settings path, and what each step costs you. Mark one done
  and Vitals measures whether it helped. Relief offers quick wins: photo clean-up, Low Power Mode in one tap, a
  restart. No fake "clear RAM" buttons.

  Hardware tests for repairs and used-phone checks: touch, display, speakers, microphones, cameras, haptics, buttons,
  sensors, Face ID, Bluetooth, GPS, NFC, flashlight, and both displays and the hinge on iPhone Duo.

  Honest by design: every value is labelled measured, from a log, or inferred. No accounts, no tracking, no ads.
  Open source.
- **Privacy nutrition label**: Data Not Collected. Photos access (Relief clean-up, optional, asked only on that
